Organisers

The people behind ICESGSD 2026

A collaboration between AIMST University, Sri Venkateswara Dental College & Hospital and Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth, supported by an international advisory network.

Host & partners

Institutions

AIMST University (Faculty of Dentistry)

AIMST University, Malaysia is a leading private university known for its strong research culture and academic excellence across health sciences. Its Faculty of Dentistry is committed to producing socially responsible, clinically competent dental professionals, and is spearheading ICESGSD 2026 as part of its drive to embed ESG and sustainability principles into dental education, research and practice across the ASEAN region.

Host institution

Sri Venkateswara Dental College & Hospital (SVDCH)

Sri Venkateswara Dental College & Hospital — guided by the philosophy “Knowledge is Power” — is a collaborating partner institution recognised for its contributions to dental education, clinical training and community oral healthcare, bringing valuable clinical and academic expertise to the conference.

Collaborating partner

Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth (SBV)

Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth is a Deemed-to-be University with a multi-disciplinary healthcare academic network. As a collaborating partner, SBV contributes an interdisciplinary perspective on health sciences education, research and governance to ICESGSD 2026.
