About the Organisers

AIMST University (Faculty of Dentistry)

Host Institution

AIMST University, Malaysia is a leading private university known for its strong research culture and academic excellence across health sciences. Its Faculty of Dentistry is committed to producing socially responsible, clinically competent dental professionals, and is spearheading ICESGSD 2026 as part of its drive to embed ESG and sustainability principles into dental education, research, and practice across the ASEAN region.

Sri Venkateswara Dental College & Hospital (SVDCH)

Collaborating Partner

Sri Venkateswara Dental College & Hospital (SVDCH) — guided by the philosophy “Knowledge is Power” — is a collaborating partner institution recognised for its contributions to dental education, clinical training, and community oral healthcare, bringing valuable clinical and academic expertise to the conference.

Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth (SBV)

Deemed to be University

Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth (SBV) is a Deemed-to-be University with a multi-disciplinary healthcare academic network. As a collaborating partner, SBV contributes an interdisciplinary perspective on health sciences education, research, and governance to ICESGSD 2026.
