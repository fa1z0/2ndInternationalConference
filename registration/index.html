Registration

Join ICESGSD 2026

Two days of keynotes, plenaries and student presentations — fully online. Choose the rate that applies to you and register before 15 September 2026 for early-bird pricing.

Fees

Registration fees

All rates are per delegate. The early-bird column is highlighted.

Delegate Before 15 September 2026 After 15 September 2026
General practitioners, specialists, academicians and others RM 100 RM 130
Students RM 80 RM 100

How to register

  1. Complete the registration form. Scan the QR code or open the form link below. Keep the confirmation email for your records.
  2. Complete payment. Use the payment portal QR code or link. Your place is confirmed once payment is received.
