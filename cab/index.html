Call for Abstracts

ICESGSD 2026 provides an interdisciplinary platform centred on Dentistry while welcoming contributions from Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Allied Health Sciences, Public Health, Biomedical Engineering, Environmental Sciences and Healthcare Management. The conference encourages research and innovations that improve sustainable healthcare through Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) principles.

ESG is not a new concept in healthcare. Every day, healthcare professionals practise ESG through infection prevention, waste segregation, sterilization, water and energy conservation, ethical patient care, digital workflows, minimally invasive treatment and the use of biocompatible materials. ICESGSD 2026 celebrates these existing practices while encouraging new ideas that strengthen sustainability, innovation and responsible governance across healthcare.

Do you have an Innovation to Share?

ICESGSD 2026 warmly welcomes innovators, inventors, researchers, clinicians, entrepreneurs and academicians who have developed novel ideas, patented technologies, published research, clinical innovations or sustainable healthcare solutions. Whether your work is at the concept stage, protected by intellectual property, translated into clinical practice or already published, this conference provides an international platform to showcase your achievements, receive expert feedback, build collaborations and inspire the global healthcare community.

Abstract Submission Instructions

  • Original Research, Clinical Case Reports and Review Articles are accepted.
  • English language only.
  • Maximum 300 words (250 for Case Reports).
  • Research abstracts: Background, Aim, Methods, Results, Conclusion.
  • Case Reports: Background, Case Description, Clinical Significance, Conclusion.
  • Maximum five keywords.
  • No tables, figures or references.
  • The Scientific Committee may allocate an Oral or Poster presentation.
