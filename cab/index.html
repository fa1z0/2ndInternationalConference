The scope

An interdisciplinary platform

ICESGSD 2026 provides an interdisciplinary platform centred on dentistry while welcoming contributions from medicine, nursing, pharmacy, allied health sciences, public health, biomedical engineering, environmental sciences and healthcare management.

The conference encourages research and innovations that improve sustainable healthcare through Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) principles.

ESG is not a new concept in healthcare. Every day, healthcare professionals practise ESG through infection prevention, waste segregation, sterilisation, water and energy conservation, ethical patient care, digital workflows, minimally invasive treatment and the use of biocompatible materials.

ICESGSD 2026 celebrates these existing practices while encouraging new ideas that strengthen sustainability, innovation and responsible governance across healthcare.

Do you have an innovation to share?

We welcome innovators, inventors, researchers, clinicians, entrepreneurs and academicians with novel ideas, patented technologies, published research, clinical innovations or sustainable healthcare solutions.

Whether your work is at concept stage, protected by intellectual property, translated into clinical practice or already published, this is an international platform to showcase it, receive expert feedback and build collaborations.

Submission instructions

Before you submit

Please follow these requirements. Abstracts that do not meet them may be returned for revision.

  • Original research, clinical case reports and review articles are accepted.
  • Submissions must be in English.
  • Maximum 300 words (250 words for case reports).
  • Research abstracts: background, aim, methods, results, conclusion.
  • Case reports: background, case description, clinical significance, conclusion.
  • Maximum five keywords.
  • No tables, figures or references.
  • The Scientific Committee may allocate an oral or poster presentation.
Ready to submit?

Send your abstract to the Scientific Committee

Email your abstract with the subject line “ICESGSD 2026 abstract submission”. The committee will confirm receipt and next steps.
